# PINTIG Feature Engineering Protocol v1.0

**Identifier:** PINTIG-FE-1.0  
**Status:** reproducible research specification; proposed features are not clinically validated.  
**Scope:** baseline answers and measurements to the 27 named model fields. Model inference stays disabled until a compatible model, endpoint and validation release exists.

## Source and implementation boundary

Thirteen supplied reference files were checksum-verified. They comprise eleven unique PDFs: `PURE.pdf` duplicates `pone.0271169.s001.pdf`, and `Physical Activity.pdf` duplicates `scoring_protocol.pdf`. The PURE form is the South African version. Generation 3 forms are US cohort instruments. Neither is treated as a Philippine-validated instrument. Exact item texts, response choices, pages, implemented skips and field identifiers are catalogued in `questionnaire_mapping.csv` and `questionnaire_dictionary.json`; executable transformations are in `src/services/featureProtocolV1.ts` and `src/services/assessment27.ts`.

Feature provenance labels mean: **observed** is directly recorded at the assessment or existing profile; **derived** is deterministically computed from those responses; **assumption-derived** additionally uses a stated convention; **unavailable** is retained as null until required source evidence exists; **synthetic** describes the separate Framingham augmentation only. Source answers are retained even if skipped after a later answer changes. Blank, explicit unknown/refusal, and skip applicability are distinct.

## Established scoring rules and explicit adaptations

IPAQ long-form MET-minutes use `MET × minutes/day × days/week`, with MET weights walking 3.3, work/leisure moderate 4.0, vigorous 8.0, cycling 6.0, vigorous yard 5.5, and indoor moderate chores 3.0. Total moderate category includes cycle and vigorous yard as the 2005 guide specifies. Current code exposes total MET-min/week in the assessment review but stores only the contracted minute predictor. IPAQ cleaning/truncation is documented in `scoring_protocol.pdf` pp10-11. This implementation recodes bouts under 10 minutes to zero, caps each reported daily duration at 180 minutes, then caps each weekly intensity-family total at 1,260 minutes (180×7); total reported daily time above 960 minutes is excluded. The intensity-family truncation is an explicit operational reading of the guide’s 180-min and 21-hours/week limit. No categorical low/moderate/high IPAQ class is substituted for minutes/week.

The published AHEI-2010 has 11 nutrient/food components, 0-110 total, including sex-specific whole-grain grams/day and nutrient measures absent from the available FFQ. JAH3 Supplement Table S1 documents AHEI, DASH and MDS component sets. Food6 borrows selected AHEI directions/thresholds but explicitly remains a PINTIG-specific six-component index on 0-100. It is not presented as an established AHEI score. Validation requires an approved full FFQ coding/nutrient recipe and local serving calibration.

The Philippines Statistics Authority urban classification is applied to an official barangay code and reference year; the 2020 census definition recognizes population, establishment and facility criteria. PINTIG records the classification provenance and never derives it from a street address. Gen3 education is a direct sociodemographic question; its normalized ordinal index is deliberately called an education proxy, not household SES.

## Worked examples

- Waist readings 82 and 84 cm: `(82+84)/2 = 83 cm`. If either answer is 0 refusal, output missing, not 41 or 42.
- Three weekly 30 mL spirits: `3×1×30×0.40×0.789 = 28.404 g ethanol/week` (assumptions apply).
- Current smoker, lifetime average 10 cigarettes/day, began age20, now age50, stopped 2 cumulative years: `(10/20)×(50−20−2)=14 pack-years`.
- IPAQ, walking 30 minutes/day on 5 days: `150 minutes/week`; MET score `3.3×30×5=495 MET-min/week`.
- IPAQ sitting 8 h weekday and 6 h weekend: `(480×5+360×2)/7=445.714 minutes/day`.
- Food6 all best-category answers score at most 66.67 under these six components because three adverse categories score zero and three adequacy categories score one; the score is not designed to mimic the synthetic 25.7-88 distribution.
- Education bachelor (code7): `100×7/8 = 87.5` education-index points. Prefer-not-to-answer code9 gives missing.
- Adult passive exposures of 10 and 8 years: known union is bounded `[10,18]`; protocol feature is conservative 10 years. If intervals later become available, recompute exact union under a new protocol version.

## Dataset compatibility audit

The frozen target file was read only: 4,191 unique participants, 33 columns (27 predictors +6 metadata), events 369 recorded-CVD proxy, 218 death-before-recorded-CVD proxy, 3,604 censored. SHA-256 and per-feature synthetic ranges/missing counts are in `dataset_review.json`. Dataset fields retain their existing values and provenance. Code/unit/nominal-range compatibility is not distributional equivalence: SES, urban/rural, diet and stress were simulated; activity is synthetic minutes with 0-900 range; passive adult years were generated as interval unions; smoking packs/intensity and alcohol came from synthetic assumptions. Clinical questionnaire transformations differ. Do not fit/use the current model with this protocol until participant-level definitions, missingness, preprocessing and prediction schema match and validation is performed.

The original dataset SHA-256 is `86dd7dca93582e9a8d67cabead1e94406e973b3e0aa9cf9febde7b8804f4ec5c`. Outcomes remain recorded-CVD and death-before-CVD proxies, not verified major CVD/non-CVD death. No outcome is used to engineer a baseline predictor.

## Provenance and version rules

Each intake record retains schema version, protocol version, full raw response map, source and applicability maps, feature map, derivation status and limitations. Missing inputs stay missing; no outcome-informed imputation occurs. Changes to question wording, skips, source reference, scoring, units or category order require a protocol version change and rerun of unit tests. Observed questionnaire answers, derived values, assumption-derived values and synthetic research data must never be relabeled as one another.
