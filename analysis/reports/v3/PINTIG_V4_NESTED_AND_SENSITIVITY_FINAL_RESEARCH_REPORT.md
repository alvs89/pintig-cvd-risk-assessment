# PINTIG Thesis 1 — semi-synthetic competing-risk RSF experiments (2026-10-31)

## Status
Focused nested 5×3 comparison COMPLETED for 4 prespecified configurations (16, smoking18, behaviour20, all26). 200 patient-level paired bootstrap replicates COMPLETED for AUC and 10-year Brier. Two additional simulation seeds COMPLETED for fixed-hyperparameter sensitivity (40 fits). The 11 counts 16–26 five-fold pilot comparison was previously completed (55 fits). All results simulation-only, not clinical predictor validation. Frozen originals and outcomes not changed.

### Provenance
Source provisional cohort: 4,191 participants, event type 1=369, event type 2 competing death=218, event type 0 censored=3,604. Existing six added baseline fields were already synthetic; **ten extra predictors are newly artificially generated** conditional on observed or existing synthetic baseline values, never based on outcome labels/times. These represent fictional illustrative patient histories; cannot infer genuine PURE predictor utility. Their exact algorithm and seed are in analysis/scripts/v3/generate_v4_simulation.py, and limitations in analysis/reports/v3/PINTIG_V4_SIMULATION_METHOD.md.

## Experiment definitions
- base16: original 16 predictors.
- smoking18: + simulated smoking_pack_years and former_smoker.
- behaviour20: + simulated sitting_minutes_day, heavy_episodic_alcohol, psychosocial_stress, sleep_duration_hours.
- all26: all ten newly simulated candidates.
- Outer folds: preserved 5 participant-level splits; inner 3-fold stratified by event type inside training only.
- RSF: randomForestSRC competing risks logrankCR with cause=1; 100 trees per inner-screen fit, 200 final per outer fit. Only 2 prespecified hyperparameter configurations considered per fold, therefore **focused, not exhaustive tuning**. Inner model selection uses unweighted Brier on cases with observable 10-year status as *screening heuristic*, not gold-standard competing-risk IPCW; revise in future strict protocol.
- Original missing-value imputation: baseline v3 script learned training-only BP-medication mode, heart-rate median, and current-smokers' cigarettes/day median; non-smoker cigarette amount structurally zero. Tested on validation folds. Does NOT perform multiple imputation. Additional simulation columns are fully generated by construction, not statistically imputed from observed data.
- Evaluation: OOF competing-risk CIF cause 1, ten time points at approx yearly intervals; horizon=3652.5 days. Competing events and censoring considered in simple IPCW estimators. AUC and Brier censor-adjusted approximations, calibration intercept/slope fitted by weighted complementary-log-log pseudo-binomial regression. Calibration slopes should be cautiously interpreted and externally verified against established competing-risk calibration methods. IBS here is trapezoidal integration **from year 1 through year 10**, not year 0 to 10, because predictions were saved only at those grid points. 200 patient-level paired bootstrap resamples of out-of-fold results yield percentile CIs conditional on fitted models and this cohort; intervals do not encompass synthetic-generation uncertainty, model development variability, or external validation.

Under the two alternate simulated draws, base16 has the smallest Brier in every scenario, and expanded models fail to show a stable predictive gain. This reverses the 18-feature nested comparison under the original draw. Models and metrics from different tuning protocols are NOT directly comparable as absolute values; the sensitivity conclusions concern within-scenario changes.

## Decision and limitations
**Do not finalize 18 or 23 predictors based on these simulations.** The data cannot show clinical incremental value of hypothetical smoking duration, former smoking, stress, etc. For Thesis 1, keep **16 as the frozen provisional reference configuration** (still includes six synthetic baseline features) and present expanded scenarios as clearly labeled simulation sensitivity experiments, not definitive predictor selection. Complete model calibration/recalibration only after real cohort data; calibration slopes ~0.24 flag substantial potential miscalibration of this experimental approach and require technical review. Any deployed clinical model must wait for provenance-verified PURE Philippines data and validation.

### Not fully completed
- Exhaustive 16–26 predictor-count **nested** evaluation: the 11 count comparison is five-fold fixed-parameter pilot only; full 5x3 was applied only to 4 prespecified group configurations.
- Repeated simulation sensitivity with *empirically justified externally observed joint distributions* rather than only alternate random seeds of the same illustrative generator.
- Multiple imputation sensitivity and full clinical prediction-quality study; structural absence of newly synthetic variables cannot be repaired by MICE.
- Full ten-year calibration plots/nonparametric Aalen-Johansen calibration, independent external validation, net benefit/decision curves (inappropriate to use simulated scores for clinical utility claims).
- Full IBS from time 0; current measured years 1 through 10.
- Uncertainty for IBS/calibration with complete model-refit bootstrap.
- Questionnaire UI updates: not implemented as these synthetic values are not deployable predictors.
